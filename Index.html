Frequently Asked Questions About Online Therapy

Quick answers regarding virtual telehealth sessions, provider matching, rescheduling policies, billing, and insurance coverage.

Our quiz connects directly to the live Guzman & Baker Provider Directory API. It strictly filters by your state of residence (for clinical licensure compliance) and your selected insurance or payment option, then ranks providers by exact clinical specialty alignment and current availability.
Current patients can click the "Current Patients" button at the top of this page to sign in to the HIPAA-compliant Patient Portal, join upcoming video sessions from any computer, tablet, or smartphone, or use Send a Secure Message to reach their therapist or our clinical care team.
Appointments can be managed through the Patient Portal or by selecting Request a Callback in the Current Patients menu with at least 24 hours' notice. For questions about insurance claims, copays, HSA/FSA cards, or superbills, choose Billing Questions in the Current Patients menu and our billing team will respond within 1–2 business days.
State licensing boards require mental health clinicians to hold an active license in the state where the client is physically located during the video session. Selecting your state in Step 1 ensures every matched provider is legally licensed to provide your telehealth care.
Clinicians displaying the green "Accepting New Patients" badge have active openings in their telehealth schedule and are prioritized in your match results so you can get started without long waitlists.
Every matched clinician card includes a direct "View Profile & Book Telehealth" button that takes you straight to their full directory profile and intake request page. Our team responds within 1–2 business days to verify your insurance and finalize your appointment.
